DashCinema

Car-screen video player and navigation for iPhone and CarPlay.

Getting started

  1. Open DashCinema on your iPhone and add a source in Channels → Add: an M3U playlist link, an Xtream login, a video link, or a YouTube link. You can also add videos from Photos or Files in Channels → My Videos.
  2. Connect your iPhone to CarPlay (wired or wireless) and open DashCinema on the car screen.
  3. Use Browse on the car screen to pick something to watch.

Safety

Video on the car screen is for passengers. While the car is moving, video appears only after a passenger confirms on the car screen. The driver must never watch video while driving. Obey local laws.

Content

DashCinema does not provide any channels, movies or other content. You add your own sources and are responsible for having the right to use them.

Subscriptions

DashCinema includes 5 hours of free watching. After that, DashCinema Pro is available monthly or yearly. Manage or cancel in Settings → Apple Account → Subscriptions. Refunds are handled by Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com.

DashCinema is tested up to the iOS version shown in the app. A future iOS or CarPlay update from Apple may block or limit video on the car screen.

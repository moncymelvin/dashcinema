Privacy Policy

DashCinema · Last updated 15 November 2026

Summary

DashCinema does not collect, store on our servers, sell or share your personal data. We have no servers and no accounts.

Data that stays on your iPhone

  • Your sources (playlist links, Xtream logins, favorites, recent channels and imported videos) are stored only on your device. Passwords are kept in the iOS Keychain.
  • Location is used on your device to show the map and directions and to tell whether the car is moving. It is not sent to us.
  • Free-time counter for the 5 free hours is stored in the Keychain on your device.
  • Diagnostics log stays on your device unless you choose to export and share it.

Third parties you connect to

When you play something, DashCinema connects directly to the source you added (your IPTV provider, media server or website), or to YouTube for YouTube videos. Those services handle data under their own privacy policies. Maps and directions use Apple Maps.

Purchases

Subscriptions are processed by Apple. We do not receive your payment details.

Children

DashCinema is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect data from anyone.
